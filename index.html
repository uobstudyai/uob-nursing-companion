UoB Faculty

Weekly fidelity audit

Rate 10 companion answers drawn at random from your course's last 7 days of student use. Student names and IDs are never shown. It takes about 15–20 minutes.

Your role during weeks 1–7

  • Each week, open this page and rate the 10 answers drawn for your course.
  • Mark each one Accurate and in scope, Inaccurate, or Out of scope. Add a note for anything that needs fixing.
  • The principal investigator corrects reported problems within 48 hours.
  • For anything urgent, email maltheeb@uob.edu.bh directly.
