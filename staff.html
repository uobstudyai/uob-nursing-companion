Weekly fidelity audit

Rate 10 companion answers drawn at random from your course's last 7 days of student use. Student names and IDs are never shown. It takes about 15–20 minutes.

Your role during weeks 1–7

  • Each week, open this page and rate the 10 answers drawn for your course.
  • Mark each one Accurate and in scope, Inaccurate, or Out of scope. Add a note for anything that needs fixing.
  • The principal investigator corrects reported problems within 48 hours.
  • For anything urgent, email maltheeb@uob.edu.bh directly.

University of BahrainStudy admin Project 25-2024

Study overview

Impact of a Customized AI Companion on Nursing Students' Motivation for Learning and Self-Directed Learning. Weeks 1–7, February to April 2027.

Study controls

Opening a questionnaire shows it to every consented student on the roster and adds a reminder in the companion until they finish. Each student can submit once per questionnaire. Also announce it on Blackboard, because Group B students may not sign in otherwise.

Pictures and videos library

Add items as rows in the Media tab of the study spreadsheet: ID (short, unique, e.g. NUR125-BP-1), Course, Topic ID (from the list below; leave blank for the whole course), Type (image, video or link), Title (what the AI sees), URL (https; YouTube links play inside the companion), Source, Licence, Approved (Y). Only approved rows reach students; changes appear within 10 minutes (immediately after you press Load here). Use only material you have the right to share, and never textbook figures from the course PDFs.
